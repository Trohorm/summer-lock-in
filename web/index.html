How Lock In grades food
Build every meal on
  • Beef, lamb and other ruminant meat
  • Eggs — whole, yolks included
  • Liver and other organs, 100–200 g a week
  • Wild fish and shellfish
  • Full-fat dairy — A2/A2 milk is best — plus butter, ghee, tallow
  • Ripe fruit, honey, maple syrup
Keep out
  • Seed oils — canola, sunflower, soybean, corn, “vegetable oil”, margarine
  • Fried and restaurant food cooked in them
  • Refined sugar and syrups
  • Ultra-processed food and refined flour
  • Alcohol and artificial sweeteners
Minimise
  • Grains, legumes, nuts and seeds — plant defence chemicals that block minerals
  • Raw leafy greens and soy
  • Chicken skin and pork fat (omega-6)

Every meal you log gets graded against these rules, with warnings and what to eat next.

Limits: seed oils, omega-6, sugar & anti-nutrients

Seed oils stay at zero. Omega-6 (linoleic acid) 7 g and refined sugar 15 g a day. Oxalates 40 mg (clinical low-oxalate diet), phytates 400 mg (keeps zinc absorbing well), solanine 0.1 mg/kg and cyanide 0.02 mg/kg bodyweight (EU food-safety thresholds); the rest are set at low everyday intakes. Blank = default.
