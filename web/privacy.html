Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Lock In is a gym and diet tracker made by an individual developer. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, or your Google account's email and ID if you sign in with Google. If you add a password, only a scrambled version of a key made from it is kept — never the password itself.
  • Your training and food data: workouts, meals, body weight, splits, progress photos and settings. They are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, so you can use them on every device you sign in on.
  • Your encryption keys are stored with your account, so signing in with your email on a new device gets everything back. This protects your data from other people, but it means it is not hidden from the Lock In server itself.
  • Friends: your name, username and profile photo are visible to people who look you up. What friends can see of your training is your choice and is encrypted for them.

What we use it for

Only to run the app: signing you in, syncing your data between your devices, and the features you use. We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores the data.
  • Google signs you in if you choose “Continue with Google” (we only receive your name and email).
  • An email provider sends sign-in codes to your email address.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads a meal photo or description and your workouts when you use AI features, to give you an estimate or feedback. That data is sent only for that request.
  • Stripe handles payments if you buy Pro (it is the seller and handles VAT); we don’t see your card details.

Deleting your data

In the app: Setup → Account & data → Delete my account removes your account, data, photos and friends profile. “Erase all data” removes just your data.

Children

Lock In is meant for people training in a gym. If you are under 16, use it with a parent's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
